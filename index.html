Cutting guides

Sizes are approximate film dimensions in mm. Print at 100% / "Actual size" (no fit-to-page). Empty frames are not printed. Corner marks use the gap between frames, so keep gap at 2 mm or more. Changing size keeps your photos and crop positions.
